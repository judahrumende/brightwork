---
name: foundable-store-dashboard
description: Build or review the store management experience that opens from the Foundable site preview.
---

Reuse Foundable controls, typography and surface tokens. Put Dashboard below the site preview. The dashboard should identify the selected store and offer a quick return to the preview. Organize management around the merchant's work in products, orders and customers. Make empty states actionable and show status, currency and permissions next to the affected records.

Use actual store records for every metric. Show the difference between an order request and fulfillment through its status and available actions. Verify store and workspace isolation, member permissions, stale edits, and competing inventory updates. Keep customer records inside the app's authenticated management surface.

Base44's [data permissions](https://docs.base44.com/Setting-up-your-app/Managing-security-settings.md) and app dashboard inform the separation between customer pages and management.
